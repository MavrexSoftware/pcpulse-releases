# Privacy policy

**PC Pulse Companion** (the Windows program) and the **PC Pulse** app (the Android app)

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Mavrex Software ("we", "us") makes PC Pulse Companion and the PC Pulse app. This page explains what the programs do with information. Questions: **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**

## The short version

- There are **no accounts**, **no ads**, **no analytics and no tracking** in PC Pulse Companion.
- **We do not receive your data.** Everything the programs handle stays on your own PC, your own phone and your own local network.
- The programs connect to the internet only to **check for updates** (GitHub), to **download things you ask for** (GitHub), and when you open a **Google Play** or **GitHub** link.
- We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

## What the programs handle on your devices

**PC Pulse Companion (on your PC)** keeps, in your user profile on the PC:

- the phones you paired: the name each phone gave, when it was last seen, and a one-way checksum of its pairing key (not the key itself);
- your settings, shortcuts and their icons, scenes, automations and display profiles;
- the program's encryption certificate and private key, which let phones recognise your PC;
- a short activity log that is kept in memory for about an hour and is not saved to disk;
- a crash report, only if the program hits an internal error. Reports contain the program version, the Windows version, the error, and recent activity with network addresses shortened. They are never sent anywhere. You decide whether to share one.

When you use a feature, the program also handles the following **only while you use it, and does not store it**: the keys and mouse movements a phone sends, pictures of your screen for the screen view, your clipboard text and files you send or receive (if you turned those features on), hardware readings such as temperatures and usage, and the list of running programs. This information goes from your PC to your paired phone over your local network.

**The PC Pulse app (on your phone)** keeps: the PCs you paired (their names, network addresses and certificate fingerprints), the pairing key for each PC (stored encrypted on your phone), and your layout and settings, for example the gamepad layout. The app uses only the permissions its features need, such as network access, Wi-Fi information to find your PC, and vibration for game feedback. To pair, you scan a QR code with Google's code scanner, which processes the camera picture on your phone. Our app does not save the picture.

## Your local network

Your phone and PC talk to each other directly. The connection between the Android app and the PC is **encrypted**, and a phone only talks to the PC it was paired with. The PC program's optional **web app** (for browsers and iPhones) is **not encrypted**, is off until you turn it on, and warns you first. You choose which features each phone may use.

## Connections to the internet

- **Update check.** About once a day the PC program asks `github.com` whether a newer version exists (you can turn this off in Settings > About and updates). GitHub receives your IP address and the usual details of a web request, and handles them under its own privacy statement. We do not receive this information. Updates are checked with a digital signature before they are installed.
- **Downloads you request.** If you click the buttons to install the ViGEmBus driver or LibreHardwareMonitor, the program downloads them from GitHub.
- **Links.** The Google Play and releases links open in your browser or in Google Play, which have their own privacy policies. Downloading the Android app through Google Play is covered by Google's terms and privacy policy.

## Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because it never reaches us. Nothing in the programs sends your data to advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties.

## Children

The programs are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Keeping your information safe

Connections from the Android app are encrypted and pinned to your PC's certificate. Pairing needs a QR code shown on your PC's screen. Pairing keys on the phone are stored encrypted. You can remove any paired phone in the PC program at any time. Nobody can protect a device that someone else controls, so keep your PC and phone locked and only pair devices you trust.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- Remove a paired phone in the PC program to cut its access.
- Turn off the update check in Settings > About and updates.
- Uninstalling PC Pulse Companion offers to delete its data folder, which removes your settings, the pairing list, the certificate and any crash reports.
- On your phone, remove a paired PC in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side. If you live where privacy laws give you rights over personal information (for example in the EU, the UK or California), those rights apply to information held by someone else, and the information on your devices is under your own control. You are welcome to contact us with any question.
